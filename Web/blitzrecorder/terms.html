Product

BlitzRecorder is a macOS recording app for creating screen, camera, and audio recordings. BlitzRecorder Camera is an iPhone companion that can pair with BlitzRecorder on Mac to provide remote camera preview, camera controls, local iPhone camera recording, and file transfer back to the Mac take.
