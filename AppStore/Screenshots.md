# BlitzRecorder App Store Screenshots

App Store screenshot assets are currently absent from this repository.
`Scripts/validate-submission-artifacts.sh --strict` reports them as missing until real captures are reviewed and added.

## Required sets

- macOS: 16:10 screenshots at an accepted App Store size.
- iPhone 6.9 inch: `1320 x 2868` or another accepted size.
- iPad 13 inch: `2064 x 2752` or another accepted size.

## Capture

`Scripts/capture-app-store-screenshots.sh --mac` captures the current macOS app UI.
Capture iPhone and iPad screens during a real pairing, recording, and transfer workflow.

Review each image before upload for accurate app state, current copy, and private information.
The screenshot directories are submission destinations, not evidence that assets have been approved.
