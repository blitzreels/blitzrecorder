# BlitzRecorder Icon Concepts

## Final Selected Icons

### macOS: Liquid Glass Capture Frame

BlitzRecorder macOS should feel like a BlitzReels-family product while using a new product-specific symbol. Use the BlitzReels emerald/dark palette, but avoid directly reusing the folded BlitzReels mark and avoid a red recording dot.

Core metaphor: a premium Liquid Glass capture frame / viewfinder for screen recording.

Visual direction:
- Near-black app-icon surface.
- Neon emerald and deep emerald glass foreground.
- A bold capture-frame or screen-frame glyph.
- Translucent layered material, frostiness, subtle specular highlights.
- Apple-native, premium, restrained pro utility feel.
- No text, no letters, no REC badge, no hardware, no screenshots, no tiny controls.

Selected source:
- `GeneratedIcons/selected/BlitzRecorder-macOS-selected.png`

Applied to:
- `Resources/AppIcon.png`
- `Resources/BlitzRecorder.icns`
- `Web/blitzrecorder/app-icon.png`

### iOS: Liquid Glass Aperture

BlitzRecorder Camera should feel like the iOS companion inside the BlitzReels family, but with a camera-specific identity.

Core metaphor: a premium Liquid Glass camera aperture / lens.

Visual direction:
- Near-black app-icon surface.
- Neon emerald and deep emerald glass lens/aperture foreground.
- Circular aperture, focus ring, or lens-prism glyph.
- Translucent glass layers, frosted refraction, soft highlights.
- Clear at small iOS icon sizes.
- No text, no letters, no REC badge, no phone hardware, no camera body, no border.

Selected source:
- `GeneratedIcons/selected/BlitzRecorderCamera-iOS-selected.png`

Applied to:
- `Apps/iOSCamera/Resources/Assets.xcassets/AppIcon.appiconset/AppIcon-1024.png`
- `Apps/iOSCamera/Resources/Icons/Icon-App-*.png`

## Brand Decisions

- Brand inheritance: BlitzReels family app.
- macOS symbol direction: capture frame / viewfinder.
- iOS symbol direction: lens aperture / camera optics.
- Finish: Liquid Glass premium.
- Palette: BlitzReels emerald/dark theme.
- Avoid: mixing down the existing BlitzReels logo with a red dot.
